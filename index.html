    FAQ

    Is this really free? What's the catch?

    Really free, unlimited scans, no account, no email capture. There's no catch — this is a static web page with no server. It costs essentially nothing to run, so it costs nothing to use.

    Where does my resume go when I click "Check my match"?

    Nowhere. The analysis runs as JavaScript inside your browser tab. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works. Close the tab and everything is gone.

    Will a low score get me auto-rejected by an ATS?

    Mostly no — the idea that ATS software auto-rejects resumes below a "score" is largely a myth. What actually happens: recruiters search and filter by keywords, and skim each resume for seconds. If the posting says "Google Analytics" and your resume says "web analytics tools", you lose both the search and the skim. That's the real problem this tool fixes.

    How is this different from Jobscan?

    Jobscan is a polished commercial product, but it costs ~$50/month after 5 free scans and requires an account. ResumeRadar covers the core use case — keyword matching against a job description — free and unlimited, without your resume ever leaving your device.

    Should I add keywords for skills I don't have?

    No. Lying on a resume backfires in interviews and on the job. Use the missing-keyword list to (a) surface skills you have but described differently, and (b) honestly assess whether the role is a fit.

    My PDF pasted as garbage text. What do I do?

    Some PDFs (especially exported from design tools) store text as images. Open your resume in Word/Google Docs and copy the text from there, or use the original document.
